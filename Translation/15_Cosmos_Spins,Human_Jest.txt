A floral shell upon Saturn’s clouds.
Across the spilled paint of the sky, who scattered so much pain, so many unhealed wounds?
Your reactions are preserved as constellations of my inward matters.
You veiled from me that one, that only heavenly glow, and in the end, time itself will strip that ring away from afar.
And in sorrow fall my tears, burning in the stratosphere, their certain fate consumed and gone.
Through a spyglass, far away, one may catch the feeble whip of a grimace, striking the lone units of nearby, passing, solitary beings.
What my eye cannot behold, My heart in dreams loses in a tide of dying excitation.
My thoughts, my ambitions, would reach the blackest matter. Empty as your words, the forces of boundless void.
And through it all, I am held by the spinning rush of your motion.
Let us once again behold those micro-elements above, whose very sight unites existence.
And no blind man is needed here to lose your depths within the light.
How easy it is to freeze in fear, when left alone before your vastness, and we attempt to chase that ungraspable ray, as it flies on, leaving us behind.
And so, to spheres kneaded out of minerals fused, You draw their atoms near, toward union in one identical, never-ending game.
It is me who conjure fractal frenzy into your time.
