A gentle breeze, the waves soft sigh.
Light nudges to the sea-ladies' lullaby.
Pure care, carefree, time flows its own way, while tides roll in and drift away.
Barrels slosh and overflow, tiny flies, lured by drink, drown below.
When storms arrive, small droplets blur the horizon's blue.
Great bolts of lightning sweep across the orbit, then thin like spilled rum, dissolving into view.
Creatures of the deep remain unmoved.
Their shapes melt into Neptune's mirror, even now, though surrounded, those phantoms drift, lost in the shadows of the waves.
To look below brings dreadful fear, yet gaze above, unblinking, and no fear appears. For there, beyond the endless blue, a greater world lies hidden from view.
While one lone fish swims in a tank upon the deck,
Like man upon the Earth, in a double cage he's kept.
A mighty treasure, concealed by human folly's gift.
Legends and chatter, dreams and monsters,
Such is the sailor's trade.
Time without end, no family near, a miracle diet of the sea.
