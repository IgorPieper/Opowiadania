I stand with a match in my hand. Watching its one and only dance. It is elusive. It burns an image into film, then vanishes from both the world and memory within minutes. It leaves behind a departing soul and wood in a state of decay. Every Object meets its end somehow. Consumed by flames, broken down by enzymes, abandoned to the mercy of radiation in the emptiness of the galaxy.
We, too, are objects. Sometimes tossed aside, burned out, playing a role of little significance. Unready for change, eventually blown out without leaving a trace in history. 
Stand here with me before the blazing furnace. Let us throw in the wood, pieces of furniture, and everything within our reach. Let us turn to ash what once delighted the eye and stood as a testament to someone's existence.
By mixing what remains with liquid, we create ink, transforming once-feuding families into a shared work of art. Take a brush in your hand and begin painting the world. You are a match, let your dance inspire wonder and color the world, even if only in the color of unity, leaving the colors of war to those whose decay does not end in a fraction of a second.
