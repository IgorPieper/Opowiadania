You lie at the bottom.
Darkness everywhere. Something tingles you.
Your figure is wrapped in blue. A distant whisper reaches you.
Unknown sounds. Bubbling, rushing, cracking.
Nothing that should form a sentence.
Your movements are so heavy, so slow.
With every blink, you struggle to lift even a gram of your eyelids.
The ground beneath you feels like sand, made of grains so fine.
When disturbed, it leaves a faint haze behind.
The sounds begin to take a certain shape.
You notice recurring fragments, catch a structure, yet cannot piece a thing together.
You lose letters, swallow syllables. In truth, the very sound keeps slipping away.
A faint shadow begins to emerge in the distance.
First one, then another. More and more streaks appear far away.
The perspective of your vision begins to bend.
Voices grow louder, while shapes begin to merge without rhyme or reason.
An inhuman calm completes the scene, closing around you like a little casket.
The water slowly turns scarlet.
Sound becomes a scream, the shapes seem torn apart… And stillness comes.
Two delicate, flawless hands of blue enfold you.
Can you hear me? Wake up.
This is no time or place to be seeing you here.
You hear everything clearly now.
Calm recedes, and determination quietly takes its place.
The filter of color fades away.
A smile comes full circle.
I'm on my way. The road leads up there, to the summit.



Though your eyes are deeply piercing,
They long to see it all.
Owl heads crown your upright frames, here in this earthly vale.
Your wings are irregular, shapeless masses, as though they were made of stone.
One smaller than the other. Almost as if someone had spilled mercury.
Your skin is beige, your heads are brown, Your wings the color of mud.
Grey-blue veins run through them.
There were two of you.
I can say no more.
